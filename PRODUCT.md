# Inspectr

## Register

product

## Users

Developers and site owners reviewing a public website before launch, typically at a desk during the working day. They need a clear list of overlooked publishing details and a practical next step.

## Product Purpose

Inspect public server HTML, response headers, and site files. Present observed evidence and issues in a readable report. Success is finding and fixing missing details without overstating what automated checks can prove.

## Brand Personality

Sleek, simple, precise. A light, minimal workspace with a restrained green accent, confirmed by the user.

## Anti-references

AI template gradients, glass panels, glowing orbs, repetitive icon cards, decorative motion, inflated claims, and fake metrics.

## Design Principles

Put the URL entry first. Prefer readable rows to decorative containers. Make issues and evidence easy to find. Explain the limits of every check. Give each action a working outcome.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic landmarks, keyboard navigation, visible focus, text status labels, responsive layouts, and reduced motion support.
